## 1. Product Hunt — launch description

**Tagline (60 симв.):** Free business calculators that don't ask for your email

**Description:**
RateKit is a set of free, no-signup business calculators for founders and freelancers — ROI, break-even, freelance rate, VAT/GST, profit margin, burn rate, SAFE equity, and more. Everything runs in your browser, nothing is stored on a server. Each tool explains the formula and has a worked example, so you're not just getting a number — you understand where it came from.

Also ships a free JSON API (openapi.json) so AI agents and scripts can call the calculators directly instead of re-deriving the math themselves.

**First comment (maker comment):**
Hey PH! I built RateKit because every time I needed to check a break-even point or convert a salary to an hourly rate, I ended up on a bloated calculator site with 8 ad networks and a popup asking for my email. RateKit is the opposite: no signup, no data collection, formulas shown right on the page. Would love feedback on what calculator to add next.

## 2. r/freelance — Reddit post

**Title:** Made a free rate calculator that shows you the actual formula (no signup)

**Body:**
I kept seeing people here ask "how do I know what to charge" so I built a small tool: you put in your target income, expenses, tax rate and work schedule, and it gives you a minimum hourly/daily rate — plus it shows the formula and a worked example so you can sanity-check it yourself instead of trusting a black box.

It's part of a small set of free calculators (VAT, invoice generator, ROI, etc) — no signup, nothing saved on a server, runs in your browser: https://ratekit.org/freelance-rate

Not trying to sell anything, just sharing since this sub is where I got the idea. Happy to take requests for what calculator to add next.
